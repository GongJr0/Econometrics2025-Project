\section{Data}\label{sec:data}
The initial model specification includes a wide range of macroeconomic indicators alongside the interactions.
Observations for all series span from 1989Q4 to 2025Q1 and the frequency is quarterly.
All series are sourced from the St. Louis Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) database.

Using the series in Table~\ref{tab:fred_series}, we construct our target variable and regressors as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Target Variable: $\Delta\log(Y_t)$
    \item Regressors:
    \begin{description}
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 1]}$ ($pce$): ] $\Delta\log(pce_{t-1})$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 2]}$ ($pdi$): ] $\Delta\log(pdi_{t-1})$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 3]}$ ($govexp$): ] $\Delta\log(govexp_{t-1})$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 4]}$ ($ugap$): ] $u_{t-1} - u^*_{t-1}$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 5]}$ ($ogap$): ] $\log(Y_{t-1}) - \log(Y^*_{t-1})$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 6]}$ ($okun$): ] $ugap \times ogap$ 
        \item [$X_{[t,\, 7]}$ ($phillips$): ] $ugap \times \Delta\log(odef_{t-1})$ 
    \end{description}
\end{itemize}

All variables that are observed in levels are transformed into $\log$ differences while both the gap variables and interactions are not differenced.
In our specification, $\log$ differencing is used to clean the regressors of slow-moving trends and potential multicollinearity; which is mostly an artifact of data in pure levels.
The gaps and interactions are already detrended to an extent by their construction.
