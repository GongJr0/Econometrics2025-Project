\begin{abstract}
This paper presents two interactions based on Okun's Law and the Phillips Curve as potential determinants of the U.S.\@ output growth.
Alongside other macroeconomic indicators, these interactions are included in an OLS-estimated linear regression.
After reaching a satisfactory model specification, several hypotheses regarding the significance and signs of the interaction coefficients are confirmed.
As a result, we observe a significant and negative coefficient for the Phillips interaction, while the Okun interaction is found to be insignificant as per our hypothesis.
\end{abstract}
